BGAA Privacy Policy

Operator: Pornnarai Tananchai · Contact: motoraathailand@gmail.com · Effective: 2 November 2026

Applies to the BGAA app (com.motoraa.bgaa) · The Thai version prevails · Prepared under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA)

1. In short

BGAA has no server of our own. We do not receive, keep or sell your personal data; the app's data stays on your phone. The app has no ads, does not use the advertising ID, has no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and does not ask for location, contacts or camera permission.

2. What the app uses, and where it stays

  • Sign in with Google: the name and email of your Google account identify you inside the app. They are kept on your phone only and are not sent to us. Google processes the sign-in under its own policy.
  • Favourites, recent plays, settings and downloaded tracks: stored on your phone only.
  • AI keys you add (optional): encrypted with the Android Keystore, stored on your phone, and sent only to the AI provider you chose, when you use that feature.
  • Photos of your venue (optional): when you tap "Find music from photos", the app shrinks the photos and removes location data on your phone, then sends them to the AI provider you chose (Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter) using your own key. The app does not keep the photos. The provider's own policy applies to its processing.
  • Playing and downloading music: audio files are fetched from public hosts (archive.org, files.freemusicarchive.org, audionautix.com). Those hosts can see your IP address and the file request, as with any web page.
  • Feedback: the feedback button opens your own email app; nothing is sent until you send it.

3. Who receives data

We do not sell your data. The only recipients are those in section 2 (Google for sign-in, the AI provider you choose, and the public audio hosts), which may be outside Thailand, for example in the USA.

4. Storage and deletion

Delete everything on the phone with Me → Delete all data on this phone, or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you on a server, so there is nothing further to delete. You can remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See Delete your data and account.

5. Music and copyright

Every track is published under an open licence (CC0, CC BY or public domain); the app shows each track's licence, creator and source. To report a problem or ask for a track to be removed, write to motoraathailand@gmail.com.

6. Your rights (Thailand PDPA)

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdraw consent. Contact motoraathailand@gmail.com. You may also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee.

7. Age

BGAA is not designed for children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes (for example when ads are added to the official release), we will say so in the app and on this page.

BGAA 隐私政策

运营者：Pornnarai Tananchai · 联系：motoraathailand@gmail.com · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

适用于 BGAA 应用（com.motoraa.bgaa）· 以泰文版本为准 · 依据泰国《个人数据保护法》（PDPA）编写

1. 简述

BGAA 没有我们自己的服务器。我们不接收、不保存也不出售你的个人数据，应用的数据保存在你的手机上。应用没有广告，不使用广告 ID，没有统计或崩溃上报工具，也不申请位置、通讯录或相机权限。

2. 应用使用的数据及其位置

  • Google 登录：Google 账号的姓名和邮箱仅用于在应用内识别你，只保存在手机上，不会发送给我们。登录由 Google 按其政策处理。
  • 收藏、最近播放、设置和已下载曲目：仅保存在手机上。
  • 你自己添加的 AI 密钥（可选）：用 Android Keystore 加密保存在手机上，仅在你使用该功能时发送给你选择的 AI 服务商。
  • 店铺照片（可选）：点击“用照片找音乐”时，应用在手机上缩小照片并去除位置信息，再用你自己的密钥发送给你选择的 AI 服务商（Google Gemini、Groq 或 OpenRouter）。应用不保存照片，服务商的处理按其自身政策执行。
  • 播放和下载音乐：音频文件从公共服务器获取（archive.org、files.freemusicarchive.org、audionautix.com）。与访问普通网页一样，这些服务器能看到你的 IP 地址和文件请求。
  • 反馈：反馈按钮只会打开你自己的邮件应用，在你亲自发送之前不会发送任何内容。

3. 数据接收方

我们不出售你的数据。接收方只有第 2 条所列的（用于登录的 Google、你选择的 AI 服务商、公共音频服务器），可能位于泰国境外，例如美国。

4. 存储与删除

在“我 → 删除这部手机上的所有数据”中可清除手机上的全部数据，或直接卸载应用。服务器上没有你的数据，因此无需进一步删除。可在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销应用对 Google 账号的访问。详见删除数据与账号。

5. 音乐与版权

所有曲目均以开放授权（CC0、CC BY 或公有领域）发布，应用显示每首曲目的授权、创作者和来源。如需举报曲目或申请下架，请联系 motoraathailand@gmail.com。

6. 你的权利（泰国 PDPA）

你可以申请查阅、更正、删除、限制处理、反对，或撤回同意。请联系 motoraathailand@gmail.com。你也可以向泰国个人数据保护委员会投诉。

7. 年龄

BGAA 不是为 13 岁以下儿童设计的。

8. 变更

如本政策变更（例如正式发布版加入广告），我们会在应用和本页面说明。
